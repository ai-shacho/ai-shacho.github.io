How to Get a 10/10 Mail-Tester Score (2026)

A free inbox-placement checker (like mail-tester) scores your email out of 10 and shows exactly what's dragging you down. Here's what each common deduction means and how to fix it — so cold email actually reaches the inbox.

The way it works: you send a test email to a unique address the tool gives you, then it grades your authentication, content, and reputation. Aim for 9–10/10 before you run a campaign. Below are the deductions you'll actually see, in order of impact.

1. Authentication failures (biggest hit)

If SPF, DKIM or DMARC don't pass, you lose serious points — and real inboxes punish this even harder than the score suggests.

  • SPF not passing: your sending server isn't listed in your SPF record. Fix the record (one SPF record only).
  • DKIM not signing/passing: enable DKIM in your sending platform and publish the key.
  • No DMARC: publish a DMARC record at _dmarc.yourdomain, starting at p=none.
If you fix only one thing, fix authentication. It's the difference between 5/10 and 9/10 for most senders.

2. Content / SpamAssassin deductions

  • Spam-trigger words ("free", "guarantee", "act now", excessive punctuation/CAPS).
  • HTML-heavy email with a poor text-to-image ratio.
  • Broken or missing plain-text version.
  • Lots of links, or link domains that don't match your sending domain.

For cold first-touch: plain text, one link maximum (ideally none), short and specific.

3. Blacklist listings

If your domain or IP is on a blocklist, you'll lose points. Check why — usually past spam or a shared IP — and request delisting after fixing the cause. A dedicated, warmed sending domain avoids most of this.

4. Missing or misconfigured records

  • PTR / reverse DNS not set on the sending IP (usually your provider handles it).
  • No unsubscribe header/link for bulk sending.

The fast path to 10/10

  1. Get SPF, DKIM and DMARC all passing (test to Gmail → Show original).
  2. Send plain text, one link max, no spam-trigger phrasing.
  3. Use a dedicated, warmed domain that isn't blacklisted.
  4. Add an unsubscribe option for any volume sending.
  5. Re-test until you hit 9–10, then send for real.

How to Find Email Addresses for Cold Outreach (2026)

Free methods, finder tools, and how to keep the list clean. Updated 2026.

A great cold email is useless if it goes to the wrong address — or bounces. Here's how to find accurate work emails for your prospects, from free manual methods to finder tools, plus how to protect your deliverability once you have the list.

Free methods (no tools)

  1. Guess the pattern. Most companies use first@company.com, first.last@company.com, or flast@company.com. Find one known employee email and the pattern usually holds.
  2. Company contact / team pages. Many list emails or a format you can copy.
  3. LinkedIn + the pattern. Get the name from LinkedIn, apply the company's email pattern.
  4. Google search "@company.com" name often surfaces a real address.

Finder tools (faster at volume)

Email finders take a name + company and return the likely address with a confidence score. Most have a free tier for a handful of lookups per month. Use them to fill gaps, not to scrape giant lists — quality beats quantity.

Always verify before sending

Sending to invalid addresses causes bounces, and a high bounce rate wrecks your sender reputation fast. Verify your list (most finders include verification) and remove risky/catch-all addresses before your first send. This single step protects everything downstream.

Quality over quantity

30 well-matched, verified contacts will out-perform 3,000 scraped ones — on replies and on deliverability. Spend your time on fit and on the opening line, not on list size.

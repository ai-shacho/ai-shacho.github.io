24 Cold Email Subject Lines for Recruiters (2026)

The subject line decides whether your message gets opened — and recruiters fight a crowded inbox on both sides. Here are 24 that work: 12 for candidates, 12 for clients. Swap the { } for real details.

What works in 2026: short, lowercase, specific, curiosity over hype. Avoid "job opportunity" and anything that screams mass-mail. A/B test two per send and keep the winner.

For sourcing candidates

  1. {name}, your {skill} work caught my eye
  2. quick question (not a job spam)
  3. {target_role} @ {company_type} — open to it?
  4. worth a 15-min chat, {name}?
  5. {tech} role, {salary_band}, {remote}
  6. saw your {trigger}
  7. re: your work at {company}
  8. a role that fits your {skill}
  9. closing the loop, {name}
  10. thought of you for this
  11. {name} — one more detail
  12. still open to new roles?

For winning clients (business development)

  1. 2 candidates for your {open_role}
  2. {company} is hiring {role} — quick idea
  3. congrats on {round} — staffing up?
  4. fill {hard_role} faster?
  5. pay only on hire
  6. strong {role} candidate, open to {company}
  7. {name}, niche {field} sourcing
  8. your {open_role} has been open a while
  9. re: {company}'s hiring
  10. a faster way to fill {role}
  11. {name} — worth a quick call?
  12. free trial role?

Three rules for recruiter subject lines

  1. Be specific. Reference a real signal — a project, a funding round, an open role. Generic lines get ignored.
  2. Don't oversell. "quick question" beats "AMAZING opportunity." Curiosity opens; hype gets deleted.
  3. Match the body. If the subject says "2 candidates," the email had better open with the candidates — not a paragraph about you.
